# Source registry

Last reviewed: 3 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta)

The site uses primary sources only for narrow regulatory statements. Links are educational references, not a substitute for checking current official text and a borrower's signed agreement.

## Reserve Bank of India

1. **Levy of foreclosure charges / pre-payment penalty on floating-rate term loans**  
   `https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/Notification/PDFs/DBC070514DF.pdf`  
   Used to support scoped discussion of bank charges for floating-rate term loans to individual borrowers. Applicability can depend on the current framework and loan facts.

2. **FAQ: Reset of floating interest rates on EMI-based personal loans**  
   `https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/FAQRFIR10012025.pdf`  
   Used for the general reset-options and communication framework. The official FAQ explains its product scope.

3. **Consumer guidance on home loans**  
   `https://systemhealth.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FS_FAQs.aspx?Id=77&fn=2754`  
   Used for general borrower education about APR, fees, margin/down payment and comparing lender terms. Some examples may be historical, so product facts must be verified independently.

## Review protocol

- Prefer the current document on the regulator's official domain.
- Record the review date and exact scope.
- Do not quote a current interest rate or state duty without fresh verification.
- Treat lender terms, tax consequences and property charges as user-supplied assumptions unless a reliable current source supports a narrower statement.
